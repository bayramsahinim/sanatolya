# Truva Atı — "Yunanlıların yıkamadığı surları Truvalılar kendi elleriyle yıktı"

Format: dikey 1080×1920, 30 fps, hedef 65–72 sn, ~150 kelime (TR).
Formül: tuhaf eylemle kanca → alay/uyarı → tırmanma → ters köşe → ironik final.
Kaynak: Vergilius, *Aeneis* II (Laokoon, Sinon, yılanlar, surların yıkılması, Kassandra);
Homeros, *Odysseia* IV.271–289 (Helen'in ses taklidi, Antiklos) ve VIII.492–520.
Not: Destan anlatısıdır; açıklamaya "efsaneye göre" notu konur, anlatıda hedge yok.

## 2. English adaptation (Just Needy kanalı için)

1. After ten years of war, the Greeks leave the enemy a giant wooden horse… and sail home.
2. The Trojans run to the beach: no army, no ships, just a horse.
3. A priest named Laocoön throws his spear into its side, and it sounds… hollow.
4. "Beware of Greeks bearing gifts!"
5. Then they find one Greek in the reeds, hands tied: Sinon.
6. Crying, he swears: "It's an offering to Athena. If it enters your city, Troy can never fall. That's why they built it too big for your gates."
7. Right then, two giant sea serpents crawl out of the ocean and strangle Laocoön. Message received: the horse is sacred.
8. So how do you get a horse through a gate it doesn't fit? The Trojans tear down their own walls.
9. Princess Cassandra screams, "There are soldiers inside!" Nobody believes her. She's literally cursed so nobody ever will.
10. That night, Helen circles the horse, imitating the voices of the soldiers' wives.
11. One soldier is about to answer, until Odysseus clamps a hand over his mouth.
12. At midnight, Sinon opens the hatch. The soldiers open the gates, and the fleet hiding behind the island of Tenedos sails back.
13. For ten years the Greeks couldn't break those walls. The Trojans did it themselves in one afternoon.

## 5. Ses

- **Anlatıcı:** erkek, 25–35 yaş, enerjik, hafif alaycı, ~145 kelime/dk (TR). Cümle arası boşluk minimum.
- **Müzik:** komik "antik" tema; pizzicato yaylılar + lir/buzuki melodisi + hafif davul. 9. satırda şenliğe dönüşür,
  10–12 gece kısmında gerilimli ve seyrek, 13'te büyük final akoru.
- **Miks:** -14 LUFS, müzik anlatının ~-18 dB altında, efektler önde.
