This prisoner is sculpting his own head out of soap and toilet paper, and gluing on real hair from the barbershop floor.
You see, this is Alcatraz, 1962. Thirty-six men had tried to escape. Every one was caught, shot, or drowned.
Frank Morris and the Anglin brothers stole spoons from the mess hall, and scraped at the concrete around the air vents behind their sinks.
Too loud? Every evening at music hour, Morris played his accordion.
Then they built a drill out of a vacuum cleaner motor, and hid the holes behind fake cardboard vents, painted to match.
Next, they stole more than fifty raincoats, stitched them into a raft, and sealed the seams on the hot steam pipes.
And to blow it up? The accordion became a pump.
A fourth man, Allen West, couldn't get his vent open in time. They left without him.
On the night of June eleventh, three dummy heads went to bed. Three men climbed over the roof, down to the shore, and paddled into the freezing bay.
All night, the guards counted three sleeping heads.
In the morning, a guard reached in to wake one up...
and the head rolled onto the floor.
They were never found.
