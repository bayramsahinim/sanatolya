Pattern of the three reference Shorts the user sent ("How One Cart Stopped an Entire Army", "This Shape Saved Roller

## Source

The episode is chapter 46 of *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* (14th c. novel), "borrowing arrows with straw boats".
It is fiction built around the Red Cliffs campaign (208); the histories credit a similar trick to Sun Quan at Ruxu (213),
whose boat took so many arrows on one side that he turned it to even the load. The video tells the novel's version
and names nobody.
