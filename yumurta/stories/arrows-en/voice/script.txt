He attacked a navy of a thousand ships with twenty boats full of straw men.

You see, his own commander wants him gone, so he orders a hundred thousand arrows in ten days. He says, "I only need three." "Fail, and you lose your head."

Then for two whole days, he does nothing. No blacksmiths, no feathers, not a single arrow.

On the third night, he wraps twenty boats in straw and fills them with straw soldiers. His friend asks, "Are we going to war, or scaring crows?"

Before dawn, a thick fog swallows the river, and he sails straight at the enemy camp, pounding war drums.

The enemy general hears an attack he can't see, and he won't risk his ships in that fog. So he does the safe thing: "Archers! Shoot at the drums!"

Thousands of arrows slam into the straw, until the boats start to tip. So he turns them around to fill up the other side.

You see, straw grabs an arrow and won't let go, and in fog, a straw man looks exactly like a real one.

As the sun burns through, his boats turn for home, so loaded they barely float, and his whole crew shouts back across the water: "Thanks for the arrows!"
